# Branding and assets policy

## Submitted emblem

The image supplied for this project appears to depict Andorra's coat of arms. It is not established by the repository as an original BASTION-KERN logo, and the project has no documented permission to use, modify, or redistribute it. Do not add it to the application, app icons, README, marketing materials, or a public release until the rights holder has confirmed permission and any applicable rules for official emblems have been checked with qualified counsel or the relevant authority.

For now, retain the existing BASTION-KERN wordmark treatment. A project-owned logo can be commissioned or created independently, with written confirmation that the designer grants the project the intended rights. Keep source files and provenance for any approved branding asset.

## Asset intake checklist

Before adding a logo, icon, font, screenshot, map style, or image:

- Record the creator, source, date obtained, and license or written permission.
- Confirm commercial, modification, app-store, and public redistribution rights if those uses are planned.
- Preserve required attribution and notices.
- Check that the asset does not contain another organization's protected insignia, trademarks, personal data, or sensitive locations.
- Keep a copy of the permission/license record with project documentation, but do not publish private correspondence or personal information.

The submitted image has not been added to the repository. This assessment is cautious visual identification, not a legal determination about the image or emblem.
